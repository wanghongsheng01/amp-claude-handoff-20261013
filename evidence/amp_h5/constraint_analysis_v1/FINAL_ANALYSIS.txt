1. Probability target and one candidate
The frozen local reference is a 2-layer 64-unit terminal-conditioned LSTM (62,501 parameters), output alphabet 20 residues plus EOS. p_H is the existing finite observation law: a sequence x of length n<48 has probability product_i p(x_i|x_<i,c) times p(EOS|x,c); n=48 has only the product of the 48 AA probabilities and is cap-censored, not EOS-terminated. Empty outcomes are part of p_H but constraints below exclude them. T=48 includes deterministic absorption after early termination. Temperature=1, no top-k/top-p. Distinguish raw logits, softmax implementation and effective numeric support in any later harness.
The target is pi_C(x|c)=p_H(x|c) 1{x in C}/Z_C(c), Z_C=P_pH(C)>0. Constraints must be fixed before outputs and use no activity labels: length, Q*=n_K+n_R-n_D-n_E+1{c=Amide}, and one group count H*=count(A,I,L,M,F,W,V,Y). Q* is an explicit nominal charge convention, not measured net charge at unspecified pH; H* is a count, not physical hydrophobicity. Histidine, Cys and Tyr ionization are not represented. No antimicrobial efficacy follows.
Construct only this candidate: a fully supported first-order Markov surrogate r(a|last,c), with fixed additive pseudocount 1/2, fitted in a possible later engineering study solely to train279. An EOS is counted only where observed under the existing cap semantics. No AR re-training, no development-based selection, no protein pretraining. This deliberately cheap proxy is not calibrated to p by construction; data modeling and p distillation are different. Exact backward DP under r on (position, accumulated Q*, accumulated H*, last residue, terminal) yields u_t=h_r(C|prefix). It is not exact under the LSTM. Every particle retains its own complete LSTM hidden/cell states; sharing a DP table does not merge neural states.
Use q_t(a|s)=p_t(a|s)u_t(sa)/A_t(s), A_t(s)=sum_b p_t(b|s)u_t(sb); at the final absorbing horizon u_T=1_C. Zeros are allowed only where no accepted continuation exists. Use joint reachability, not a product of separate marginal feasibility masks. No empirical flooring of a feasible u to zero; use log-domain arithmetic. All-invalid or nonfinite states are recorded failures.

2. Correctness and what it does not establish
With gamma_t(s)=p(prefix)u_t(s), the incremental SMC weight is p_t(a|s)u_t(sa)/(q_t(a|s)u_(t-1)(s))=A_t(s)/u_(t-1)(s). For resampling at every step with conditionally unbiased offspring counts, full support, fixed N and known u_0, Zhat=u_0 product_t mean_i w_t^i is unbiased for Z_C. ESS-triggered resampling must use the standard general normalizer recursion; copying the every-step product into skipped-resampling code is wrong. The safe full-path IS identity is E_q[1_C p_H/q]=Z_C. It does not require u to be calibrated. It does require the actual proposal probability including all masks and mixture factors.
Unbiased Z estimation does not imply unbiased log Z, unbiased self-normalized expectations, exact iid output sequences, or guaranteed finite-N efficiency. Resampling adds dependence and genealogical collapse; post-resampling ESS=N cannot certify diversity or fidelity. MH is another known correction: valid proposals plus the full forward/reverse proposal ratio preserve pi_C as a stationary law; finite burn-in is not exact sampling and no generic fast mixing guarantee follows.

3. Conditional approximation bound (not established for this model)
Let h_t=P_pH(C|prefix). If for every reachable feasible prefix exp(-delta_t)h_t <= u_t <= exp(delta_t)h_t, with exact zeros and u_T=h_T=1_C, then q_t/k_t* lies in [exp(-2delta_t),exp(2delta_t)], where k_t*=p_t h_t/h_(t-1). For the sequential proposal law q, not the finite-N resampled output law, D=2 sum_(t=1)^T delta_t bounds both absolute log density ratio and either KL direction; TV(q,pi_C)<=tanh(D/2). These are standard consequences of an assumed uniform relative-error certificate, not a novel guarantee derived from finite-state compression.
If every reachable suffix transition had |log(p/r)|<=epsilon, including EOS with the same cap rule, one sufficient bound is delta_t <=(T-t)epsilon. A naive substitution at T=48 gives D<=2256epsilon, but this is unnecessarily loose when u is an exact r-DP; the sharper standard telescoping bound below supersedes it for that special assumption. No such certificate has been measured or proved. Marginal calibration, held-out average NLL, correlation with success, or a confidence interval for an average cannot substitute for simultaneous prefixwise relative accuracy. Additive errors divide by h near rare constraints. Finite-state counterexample: prefixes AKK and KAK have identical length, counts and final K, yet a neural AR can assign arbitrarily different probabilities to the required next residue. DP cannot collapse them as equivalent p states.

4. Prior-art falsifier
Ctrl-G (NeurIPS 2024), Eq.1/Alg.1, already multiplies p_LM next-token probabilities by exact HMM+DFA completion probabilities. First-order r is a smaller tractable special case. Twisted SMC (ICML 2024) supplies future-potential twists and corrected particle inference. Loula et al (ICLR 2025) already offers programmable domain potentials/proposals, local normalization correction and adaptive resampling including molecular examples. Grammar-aligned MCMC (2025 preprint) directly targets constraint-conditioned laws. LSSAMP demonstrates AMP sequence/structure control; it is not a matched same-AR sampling baseline and needs different training assets. Replacing generic automata with bounded residue counts and adding standard corrections does not establish a new mechanism.
Potential practical hypothesis only: a small-alphabet joint count DP might yield a cheaper useful twist than learned CTL at a fixed workload. It may also cost more than rejection because this AR is tiny, the events may be common, and r may give misleading prefix rankings. Any observed win would first be a known-method specialization/engineering observation, not proof of innovation or biology.

8. Construction and calibration costs
For length/counter/last-token DP, a dense upper bound at horizon48 is(49)*(97)*(49)*(21)=4,890,837scalar states per terminal/constraint,about39.1MB in float64, and at most102,707,57721-way transition contributions before pruning; overhead and additional arrays are extra. These are arithmetic bounds, not measured runtimes. Lower maximum length and reachable-state pruning can reduce this. Multiple exact amino-acid composition counters multiply the state space; full20-dimensional composition is not claimed cheap. The sole candidate is limited to one declared residue-group count jointly with nominal charge and length. Independent probability approximations for charge and composition would ignore their dependence and are not this candidate.
The independent mathematical auditor supplied a sharper standard telescoping bound for the stronger uniform per-transition assumption. Since u is exact r-DP, G_t=A_t/u_(t-1)=E_{r(.|prefix,C)}[p/r] lies in exp(+-epsilon). Also Z_p/u_0=Z_p/Z_r lies in exp(+-T*epsilon). Therefore Q/pi_C=(Z_p/u_0)/product_t G_t lies in exp(+-2T*epsilon), so TV(Q,pi_C)<=tanh(T*epsilon) and either KL<=2T*epsilon. At T48 this is TV<=tanh(48epsilon), about0.048 when epsilon=0.001. A sufficient epsilon for TV<=0.1 is about0.00209, not the overly restrictive value inferred from the naive bound. This is an elementary consequence of the assumed uniform transition ratio and exact surrogate recurrence, not a new mechanism or an established guarantee for the actual trained LSTM. It remains a bound on the independent proposal Q, not the finite-particle SMC output law. We do not reject the opportunity merely because a loose bound looks vacuous: the direct prior-art overlap and the absence of an instantiated uniform certificate are the actual grounds.
The surrogate is permitted to be a heuristic without calibration for importance-correction validity. If calibration itself becomes the scientific claim, new held-out model-generated completion observations and their independence/selection protocol would be needed; that is new sampling and has not been authorized or performed in this analysis. A finite calibration sample cannot establish a universal prefixwise bound without additional structural assumptions.
